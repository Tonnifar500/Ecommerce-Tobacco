Good to know

Tasting notes
Cream and honey on the first puffs, then light cedar and a soft finish. No sharp edges and no bitterness when smoked slowly.
Origin story
Casa Aurelia is a small family-run farm in Colombia’s coffee highlands, where leaves are dried slowly in shaded barns and aged for over a year before rolling.
How to enjoy it
Clip the rounded end with a cutter, toast the foot with a match, then take slow puffs, about one a minute. Never inhale. Put it down when it stops tasting good; there are no rules about finishing it.
Storage tips
Keep it somewhere cool at 65–72% humidity, ideally in a small humidor. For a few days, a zip bag with a damp sponge nearby is fine.
