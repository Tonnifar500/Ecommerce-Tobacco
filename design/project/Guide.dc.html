A cigar or a cigarillo?

A cigar is rolled from whole tobacco leaves and is meant to be sipped slowly, never inhaled. It usually takes 30 minutes to two hours. A cigarillo is a small, thin cigar, often machine-made and flavoured, and is over in about 10 minutes.

First Draw sells premium cigars: hand-rolled, aged and kept in a humidor. They’re about taking your time, not about nicotine hits. Take the smoke into your mouth, taste it, and let it out.

Strength, from mild to full

Strength is how much body and punch a cigar has. For a first cigar, start at 1–3. You can always go stronger next time, but you can’t un-feel a cigar that was too much.

How long will it take?

Bigger cigars last longer. Give yourself time and don’t rush; a cigar that’s puffed too fast turns bitter.
