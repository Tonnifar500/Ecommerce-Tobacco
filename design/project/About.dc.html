About First Draw

We remember being beginners.

First Draw is a small Danish team of enthusiasts who still remember our own first cigar: the nerves, the jargon and the fear of doing it wrong. We built the shop we wished we’d had.

Photo slot – the team around a wooden table with coffee and a few cigars, warm evening light
How we select

Taste-tested, beginner-friendly first

Every cigar is smoked by at least three of us before it goes on the shelf. We rate strength, smoothness and how forgiving it is. If a beginner wouldn’t enjoy it, we say so on the page.

How we source

Small farms we know by name

We work with a handful of family farms and rollers, so we can tell you where each cigar comes from and who made it.

How we store

Humidor-fresh, every time

Cigars live in our humidor room at about 70% humidity until the moment we pack your order.

Our promise

Honest guidance. Fresh cigars. No snobbery.

  • We describe every cigar plainly, including who it isn’t for.
  • If a cigar arrives damaged or dry, we replace it.
  • We check age on every order, because it’s the right thing to do.
  • No question is too basic. Ask us anything.
